**"AI Life Optimizer" vs the data.** Users and buyers both behave as if Ripple is a planner you talk to (section 5). The optimiser story (habits, Life Matrix, insights) is the retention depth. The planner story is the door in. Keep both, in that order: sell the list, keep them with the patterns.

**Young ambitious adults.** The men's BWK lane is the live test. It gets paid trials at half the cost of the women's lane (`PROGRESS.md` 10-02). Next week's audit can answer this only if the collector splits trials by funnel (blind spot).

**Most defensible business.** Unchanged: months of someone's spoken life. That only exists for people who record more than once.

## 11. Risks

- **New: account pre-takeover** on journal accounts (section 6.2). This is the most serious open security issue in the repo.
- **New: Google Safe Browsing flag** on goripple.io (`PROGRESS.md` 10-03). Reviews usually clear in 1–2 business days after a clean fix ([zenweb](https://zenweb.my/blog/landing-page-disapproved-google-ads/)). Until then, Chrome users and Meta ad review may see a warning. Don't bring back in-app-browser escapes.
- **Worse: Meta Pixel on journal pages.** Unchanged in code, but 4x the ad traffic now flows through it.
- **New: email ownership is no longer checked** (`870d872`). Beyond the takeover risk, anyone can create an account under someone else's address, and lifecycle emails will go to that person.
- **Apple external links.** Still 0% commission in the US; Apple's proposed 15/10/5% is pending in court ([techcrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/14/apple-proposes-to-take-a-15-cut-of-purchases-made-outside-the-app-store/)). Ripple doesn't link out from the app yet, so there's no exposure.
