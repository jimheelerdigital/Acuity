## 2. The One Big Move

**Win back the 5 declined trial cards, and stop the next batch before it starts.**

**Why this, and not something else.** These 5 people did the hard part. They paid for ads to reach, signed up, entered a card and didn't cancel. Then the charge failed. Getting them back costs no ad money. Recovering even 2 is a 50% increase in this cohort's payers.

The current failure path loses them almost by design:
- **The email gives the wrong information.** `emails/payment-failed.ts:33` says "this month's subscription" and "your account is still active for now". The webhook sets the user to FREE immediately on the first failure (`stripe/webhook/route.ts:743-801`). So she's told everything is fine, and then finds out it isn't.
- **The link hits a sign-in wall.** The CTA goes to `/api/stripe/manage`, which redirects anyone not signed in to `/auth/signin` (`manage/route.ts:36-39`). Funnel buyers live in the app, so most of them aren't signed in on the web. Stripe's invoice has a `hosted_invoice_url` that needs no sign-in.
- **The email goes out once.** After the first failure the user is FREE. The `subscriptionStatus: { not: "FREE" }` guard then skips every retry, so they never get a second email (`route.ts:759-766`).
- **Bad cards get in at the start.** Stripe Radar has a **free trial abuse** control. It scores the card when the trial starts and blocks the trial if the first charge is likely to fail. Checkout Sessions with `trial_period_days` are detected **with no code change** ([Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/radar/free-trial-abuse)). Ripple uses exactly that (`api/onboarding/create-checkout/route.ts:98,120`). There's a backtest to review before you turn it on.
- **Meta learns from these failures.** Purchase fires at trial start (`028bdce`). A declined card counts as a "win", so Meta goes looking for more people like her.

**The move:**
1. **Today, Keenan, 30 min, no code.** In Stripe, open each of the 5 past-due subscriptions. Copy the invoice's payment link and send a personal note: "Your card didn't go through. Here's a one-tap link to fix it, no sign-in needed." Mention one thing Ripple already caught for her, if she recorded.
2. **Today, Keenan, 15 min.** Stripe → Radar → Risk controls. Review the free trial abuse backtest, then turn it on if it would have flagged the decliners and not the payers.
3. **This week, about 3 h of code (Prompt 1).** Make the failed-payment email accurate for a first post-trial charge and link it to the hosted invoice. Send 3 touches across Stripe's retry window. Count declines as "not converted" in AdLab trial quality and in the audit collector.

**Expected result.** 2 of the 5 recover. The next cohort's declines are flagged or blocked at trial start instead of at day 7.

**How we'll know in 7 days:**
- `stripe.subscriptions.past_due` drops below 3 without a matching rise in `canceled_this_week`
- `failed_charges` is under 5
- Radar's Risk controls page shows how many trial starts it blocked
- The collector's `trial_to_paid_7d` stops counting past-due subscriptions as converted (`collect-metrics.ts:595`)
