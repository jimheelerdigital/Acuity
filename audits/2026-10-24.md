## 2. The One Big Move

**Count every ad dollar before deciding to scale.**

**What's wrong.** The audit and AdLab only count spend on ads that AdLab manages. The sync loops over AdLab ad rows only (`apps/web/src/app/api/admin/adlab/cron/route.ts:172-215`), and the collector sums that table (`scripts/audit/collect-metrics.ts:443`). Three things are missing:
- **UGC creator ads.** They brought 57 of the 111 signups (`by_utm_source_medium`: ugc/creator). `PROGRESS.md` 10-20 says "UGC ads aren't AdLab rows".
- **The iOS app-install campaigns.** They went live 10-23 at $60/day for women and $25/day for men (`PROGRESS.md` 10-23).
- **The consolidated ad sets.** The new women's set ($75/day) and men's set ($40/day) only show up where they hold AdLab rows (`PROGRESS.md` 10-20).

From the budgets, this week's real spend was about **$650–900**, not $233 (estimate, unverified). That puts the full monthly budget near **$6,000** a month. For comparison, Ripple MRR is **$250** (`revenuecat.overview.mrr`) and known overhead is **$1,802** a month (sum of `costs.infrastructure_cost_table_rows`).

**Why this, and not a product fix:**
- **The scale decision is already booked.** `PROGRESS.md` 10-23 schedules "~10-30: compare cost per paid trial … ≤$20 goal, $30 OK". With AdLab-only spend, cost per trial will read about 3–4× too low. That points straight to "scale".
- **It's the biggest money decision in the company.** At $200/day, ads cost about 24× Ripple's MRR. Heeler's $1,699/month covers overhead, not ads (`stripe.other_business_revenue`).
- **The payer math is close to the line.** Charged card trials convert at 39% over 30 days (16/41, `stripe.subscriptions.trial_to_paid_30d`). Revenue per payer is about $7.14 a month (RC MRR $249.97 ÷ 35 actives). If a trial costs $31–43, a payer costs about $55–110, which is 8–15 months of revenue. One honest table decides whether scaling makes or loses money.

**The move (Prompt 1, Jimmy, about 3h):**
1. Pull spend for the whole Meta account, per campaign, every day. Include UGC, app-install and AdLab campaigns. Show it in AdLab and in the audit data file.
2. Join spend to outcomes by campaign:
   - Web: signups, paid trials and charged payers from `signupUtm*` / `utm_term` (ad set id) and Stripe.
   - iOS: App Store trials and conversions from RevenueCat, set against app-campaign spend for the same week (SKAdNetwork won't give per-user matches).
3. Decision rule for 10-30: keep a campaign only if its **cost per charged payer is ≤ $60** (about 8 months of revenue). Judge trials only until a campaign has 5+ trials that have ended.

**Expected result.** A table with one row per campaign: spend, trials, payers and cost per payer. The 10-30 decision then rests on real money.

**How we'll know in 7 days:** next week's `marketing` block shows account-level spend within 5% of Meta Ads Manager's weekly total, with a cost per paid trial for each of the three lanes.
