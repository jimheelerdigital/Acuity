## 2. The One Big Move

**Close the signup account-takeover hole today.**

**What's wrong.** If an account has no password (Google, Apple or the new 6-digit email code), anyone can "sign up" with that email. The server writes their password onto the existing account:
- web: `apps/web/src/app/api/auth/signup/route.ts:122-129`
- mobile: `apps/web/src/app/api/auth/mobile-signup/route.ts:92-96`

No proof of email ownership is asked for. Then the attacker signs in, because the credentials login doesn't check verification (`lib/auth.ts:123-131`).

It works the other way too. An attacker signs up first with a victim's email (auto-verified, `signup/route.ts:150`). When the victim later uses Google, their account merges into the attacker's because of `allowDangerousEmailAccountLinking` (`lib/auth.ts:54,73`). The attacker's password keeps working. That half has been open for three weeks (R-2026-10-03-2, R-2026-10-10-3).

**Why this, and not something growth-related:**
- **It takes 30 seconds and one email address.** No phishing, no guessing.
- **More accounts are exposed every week.** Passwordless sign-in shipped on 10-14 (`4106aa3`). This week 16 of 48 signups came through email-code, Apple or Google (`signups_breakdown.by_method`). Every one of those accounts can be taken over this way.
- **The damage is bigger than before.** Since 10-14, a Pro account can connect any MCP client to read its journal for 90 days (`cd00db7`, `lib/mcp-oauth/server.ts:275-283`). An attacker who gets in can set up their own ongoing feed of the victim's journal.
- **One incident ends the company.** Ripple's audience is women writing about overwhelm, loss and isolation (section 5). The fix is about 3 hours.

**The move (Prompt 1, Jimmy):**
1. Signup on an existing email never writes a password. It returns "account exists, sign in with your code", and setting a password requires a fresh email code.
2. Stop auto-verifying new password signups. Keep sign-in working without verification (Keenan's no-gate decision, 2026-09-28), but don't let unverified accounts be linked to by OAuth.
3. When Google or Apple links to an unverified password account, clear the password and sessions.
4. Make the email-code attempt counter atomic. Add a per-email limit (`lib/email-code.ts:102-108`, `verify/route.ts:45`).

**Expected result.** There's no path from "knows an email" to "reads the journal".

**How we'll know in 7 days:**
- A scripted check against prod: signup with a Google-only test account's email returns 409 and doesn't change it.
- Zero `passwordHash` writes on existing users outside the code-verified path (new `password_set` OnboardingEvent with `proof: code|oauth`).

Impact H (existential risk) · Effort 3h · Confidence H (read directly in code).

**Core loop:**
- record → transcribe → extract: **healthy, and fixed this week.** 87 of 89 COMPLETE. Long debriefs were hitting the 2,048-token cap mid-JSON and landing PARTIAL with no tasks. The cap is now 8,192 (`97d70be`, `packages/shared/src/constants.ts:407`).
- first record: **better.** Activation is 27.1%.

**Top issues:**
1. **Account takeover via signup (critical, new).** See section 2: `signup/route.ts:122-129`, `mobile-signup/route.ts:92-96`, `lib/auth.ts:54,73`.
2. **The MCP OAuth server has no rate limits (H).**
   - `/api/oauth/register`, `/api/oauth/token` and `/api/mcp` are all unthrottled.
   - Anyone can register unlimited clients (`register/route.ts:22`), and every `search_journal` call runs a paid embedding.
   - Consent phishing: a client can call itself "Claude"; the only tell is a small return-host line (`app/oauth/authorize/page.tsx:137`).
   - A reused refresh token is logged but not revoked (`lib/mcp-oauth/server.ts:258-266`).
   - The good parts are solid: PKCE S256, hashed single-use codes, hashed tokens, a consent screen, and Pro re-checked per request.
3. **iOS new users see two permission screens before any value.**
   - Cold start sends non-Pro users into legacy onboarding (`apps/mobile/lib/onboarding-v10/entry-routing.ts:128`): Privacy, then Mic, then Practice (`components/onboarding/index.tsx:55-61`).
   - 147 of 219 app signups in 30 days never got past step 0 (`app_onboarding_steps_last_30d`).
   - The v10 paywall still charges nothing (`onboarding-new/_v10/paywall.tsx:110-127`), and production doesn't set `EXPO_PUBLIC_ONBOARDING_V10` (`apps/mobile/eas.json:37-54`).
4. **No come-back hook is switched on.**
   - The day-2 push is built, with compliant copy ("Anything still taking up space?"), but gated off (`ENABLE_V10_DAY2_PUSH`, `inngest/functions/v10-day2-push-cron.ts:24`).
   - The weekly digest is flag-gated (`weekly-digest.ts:66-98`).
   - No streak freeze, no "30 days ago".
5. **The payment-failed email is false (week 2).** It says "still active" while the user is set to FREE at once. It links to the sign-in-walled portal, not `hosted_invoice_url`, and only one email ever goes out (`emails/payment-failed.ts:28-35`, `webhook/route.ts:759-801`).
6. **Unlogged AI cost.**
   - `pipeline.ts` and the four features moved to Opus 5.5 don't write to ClaudeCallLog.
   - `compute-user-insights` now runs Opus 5.5 weekly for every TRIAL and PRO user (`compute-user-insights.ts:171`).
   - User COGS still reads $0 (`claude_call_log.user_attributed_usd`).
7. **Email code brute force (M).**
   - The attempt counter is read, then incremented, not atomically (`lib/email-code.ts:102-108`).
   - The limit is per IP only, with no per-email cap.
   - Re-sending resets the tries, which gives about 25 guesses an hour per email.
   - The rate limiter fails open without Upstash (`lib/rate-limit.ts:270`).

## 10. Strategic Direction

**"AI Life Optimizer," tested against this week:**
- Users use Ripple as a **quick household and work capture tool**: 43-second median, errands and admin rising.
- A minority layer in **body and energy**, at 5 weeks running.
- Optimizer depth exists in the product (Opus 5.5 insights, Life Audit, State of Me). But D7 at 2.7% means almost nobody stays long enough to see it.

The thesis is a fine destination. The front door is "the list that writes itself", and the bridge is a week-2 energy pattern.

**Young ambitious adults.** The men's lane keeps winning on trial starts (4 of 7 this week, 9 of 12 last week). The themes give it some support too: career up 150%, but from 2 users only. Charged conversions per lane still aren't in the data (Blind Spots). Don't retarget the brand. Keep the men's lane as a separate funnel and judge it on charged payers.
