## 2. The One Big Move

**Make the first recording happen inside the ad funnel, before anyone sees an App Store badge.**

**Why this.** The funnel gets people to an account: 294 visitors → 18 accounts → 3 card trials (`web_funnel_events_this_week`, Stripe). Then it lets them go. After checkout, or after skipping the paywall, the last screen is `DownloadScreen` (`apps/web/src/components/onboarding-funnel.tsx:2539`). That screen puts the App Store badge first and "Continue in the Web App" second.

- 8 people reached that screen.
- 5 tapped the App Store badge and 3 tapped web.
- 1 person recorded, out of 23.

The iOS app then opens at a sign-in screen, and 20 of the last 30 days' signups sit at onboarding step 0 (`app_onboarding_steps_last_30d`).

**The fix already exists.** `FirstDebriefFlow` (intro → record → processing → extraction → CTA) is in `apps/web/src/app/auth/signup/success/first-debrief-flow.tsx:59`. It posts to `/api/record` (line 373) and shows the extracted tasks. Right now only the old `/auth/signup` path uses it; the paid funnel never does. Wire it in after checkout and after skip, and keep the download screen as the CTA at the end.

**The catch.** The microphone does not work inside Instagram or Facebook's in-app browser on iOS ([Apple forums](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/699479), [magicbell](https://www.magicbell.com/blog/pwa-ios-limitations-safari-support-complete-guide)). 27 visitors this week were flagged in a webview (`funnel_webview_detected`). For them, reuse the existing `WebviewBreakout` "open in Safari" card (`onboarding-funnel.tsx:2640`) and make the App Store the fallback. Everyone else records on the page.

**Expected result.** Activation goes from 4% to 30%+ of funnel signups. Card trials that have recorded convert; card trials that haven't get cancelled at day 4, when the reminder email lands.

**How we'll know in 7 days:**
- `funnel_first_debrief_completed` (new event) fires for 8 or more of roughly 20 funnel signups.
- `supabase.activation.pct` is above 30%.
- The first card-trial cohort's trial→paid shows up in `stripe.subscriptions.trial_to_paid_7d`.

Impact H · Effort 5 h · Confidence M. The flow exists; the webview split is the risk.
