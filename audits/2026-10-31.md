## 2. The One Big Move

**Bring back the first debrief on the web, right after payment, with typing as the default.**

**What's wrong.** On 10-23 the post-payment web debrief was removed and payers were sent to the app (`a3ce433`). Since then:
- D1 retention fell from 8.4% to **1.0%** (1/104). The cohort window, 10-23 to 10-30, starts the day of the change (`retention.d1`).
- Activation fell from 26.1% to **20.4%** (`activation`).
- /start-test: 70 signups, 9 paid trials, **5 recorded, 5 signed into the app** (`lane_funnel_split`). App-first signups: 20 signups, 17 recorded.
- On 10-27 the team found that 0 of 4 web payers had reached the app, and rewrote the instructions (`PROGRESS.md` 10-27). This week's 5/9 says the fix helped only partly, at best.

**Why typed:** the reason for removing the web debrief was that Instagram and Facebook in-app browsers block the mic (`a3ce433`). The component that was removed already supports typing. It has a "Rather type it?" box, and typed text goes through the normal pipeline (`apps/web/src/components/funnel-first-debrief.tsx:21-22, 358-384`). So the fix is to put it back with the text box first and the mic second. Mostly re-wiring, not new code.

**Why this, and not ads or price:**
- Paywall → payment is steady at 15% (16/105 vs 13/78, `web_funnel_events`). The paywall isn't the leak.
- Card trials still charge at 41–45% (`stripe.subscriptions.trial_to_paid_*`). But a payer who never records will cancel in month 2. Recording before the first charge is what lifted conversion in early October (R-2026-10-03-1).
- Web is 84 of 108 signups (`signups_breakdown.by_platform`). Every web visitor goes through this one step.

**Expected result:** /start-test "recorded" at or above "paid trials". D1 back above 8%.

**How we'll know in 7 days:** next week's `lane_funnel_split` shows recorded ≥ paid_trials for /start-test, and `funnel_first_debrief_submitted` reappears in `web_funnel_events`. Prompt 1 adds a `paid_and_recorded` column so the overlap is exact.

Impact H · Effort 2h · Confidence M-H. The cause is inferred from timing and the lane split. The data can't yet show payer-level overlap.

## 11. Strategic Direction

**"AI Life Optimizer", tested against this week:** The pieces got built: streak freezes, Insights v2, Wrapped, the month-ago card and widgets (`PROGRESS.md` 10-27, 10-28). People still don't use it like that. 11.8% record on 3+ days, the median entry is 30 seconds, and the main jobs are lists and admin. The thesis is fine as a roadmap. As a message, "planner you talk to" is what's converting.

**Young ambitious adults:** the men's lane got worse. /start-test-bwk had 18 signups, 1 paid trial, **0 recorded** and 1 app sign-in (`lane_funnel_split`). The women's lane had 70, 9, 5 and 5. Last week they were even. There's no case for retargeting the brand. If the men's lane's AdLab cost per payer is over $60, cut it.

**Most defensible business:** a household's running list of tasks, habits and patterns that the user owns and that connects to their calendar and AI tools. That only becomes defensible once people stay past week 1, and they don't yet: the D7 window is 2.4%.

**What Keenan is probably wrong about: that the paywall and the price are the levers.**
- This week had 19 paywall or price commits, a price test, 11 creator commits and 11 Mythicals commits (git log since 10-25).
- Meanwhile paywall → payment held at 15%. The leak is after payment: 5 of 9 payers at most reached the app. Before that, 76% of hook viewers leave on screen 1.
- A cheaper price makes the after-payment problem more expensive. A payer who never opens the app at $6.99 is still a refund or a cancellation.
- **Cash matters more than it did.** Burn is about $6,400 a month against $310 of MRR, now that $1,500 of retainer income is gone.
