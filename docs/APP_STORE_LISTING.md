## 4. Description (both stores)

```
You've tried the planner. Maybe four of them. Ripple is the one you just talk to.

Say what's on your plate, out loud, whenever it's on your mind. Ripple turns it into your to-do list, tracks the habits you mention, notices your mood, and shows you what keeps coming up week after week.

WHAT YOU GET
• Your to-do list, pulled out of what you said, with dates when you mention them
• Habit tracking without ticking boxes: say you went for a walk and it counts
• Patterns: the things that keep coming up, in your own words
• A weekly report of what moved, what slipped and what's next
• Your life across six areas, so you can see where you're thriving and where you're stretched

HOW IT WORKS
1. Tap record and talk. No typing, no blank page, no right way to do it.
2. Ripple sorts it into tasks, habits and themes.
3. Come back whenever something's on your mind. The more you talk, the more it notices.

WHO IT'S FOR
People carrying a lot: work, family, the calls you keep meaning to make. If your only system is remembering, Ripple remembers for you.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your debriefs are yours. No public feed, no sharing, and you can export or delete everything at any time.

PRICING
Start with a 7-day free trial. Then $9.99 a month, or $89.99 a year. Cancel anytime.

Ripple is a personal planning and reflection tool, not a medical or mental-health service.
```
